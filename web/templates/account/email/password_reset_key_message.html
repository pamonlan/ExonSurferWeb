Welcome to MIO: MicroRNA target analysis system for Immuno-Oncology.
MIO is your gateway to identify miRNA target genes associated with immune-related signatures and pathways in cancer samples. We've developed a user-friendly web platform and the Python toolkit miopy, integrating various analyses and visualization methods based on provided or custom bulk miRNA and gene expression data. Our platform includes regularized regression and survival analysis and provides information from 40 miRNA target prediction tools. Additionally, we offer a collection of curated immune-related gene signatures and analyses for over 30 cancer types, all based on data from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA).
Don't forget to cite our work:
Pablo Monfort-Lanzas, Raphael Gronauer, Leonie Madersbacher, Christoph Schatz, Dietmar Rieder, Hubert Hackl, MIO: microRNA target analysis system for immuno-oncology, Bioinformatics, Volume 38, Issue 14, 15 July 2022, Pages 3665–3667, 10.1093/bioinformatics/btac366. PMID: 35642895.
